Sean Golder

Mathematics and Physics
Schreyer Honors College, Pennsylvania State University

I am a senior in Penn State's Schreyer Honors College. In May 2027 I will complete B.S. degrees in Mathematics (Honors) and Physics, minors in Spanish and History, and an M.S. in Physics.

I am interested in the mathematical structure of physical theories, especially quantum mechanics on curved spaces, which draws on geometric analysis and spectral theory. In my current research with Dr. Christopher Griffin at Penn State, I study quantum dynamics on statistical manifolds, spaces of probability distributions with a natural Riemannian geometry. Our first paper, Classical and Quantum Dynamics in an Information Theoretic Space, has been revised and resubmitted to the Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical.
